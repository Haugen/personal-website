Lots of interesting career things to consider over the weekend. What do we focus on moving forward? Where do we see ourselves in the future?

## Staff Engineer

Obviously, our main focus will be the new role as Staff Engineer at Volvo Cars. Stepping into a new, senior position, tackling challenging technical as well as organizational challenges. Explore, seek mentors, read. Find ways in your day to day to establish and keep good habits. Don't stress, but take your career seriously.

## Golang

I've lost the focus on Go a bit. This still feels like an exciting new technology to focus on. Build things, learn, share. Make content, both in writing and on youtube. There's definitely somethere here, both for the work and future at Volvo, but as well as the future of your career.

## Make content

Twitch and Youtube. Streaming, writing, making video content. There's still something there. Can't let it go. Beautiful to see how it can help people. Strangers online. Also, if I do less teaching at Yrgo moving forward, this could be a great substitute.

## Accessibility focus

Interesting discussions with Blake this weekend, around the new accessibility act coming in EU next year. Potentially a big opportunity to position oneself as an expert in the area. Do both consulting, but also possibility to start and grow a company. Must explore idea, for sure.

## Wrap up

In the end, as so often, we need focus. We need to pick something, and give it time. In the meantime, let other things go. Don't stress over everything. That will harm the results. Years will keep going by. We can do great things, if we do them one at a time.

So what do we do? While writing this, I don't know. Focus on Staff at Volvo. Focus on yourself. Your health, your habits, your relationships. Investigate the other possibilities, especially around accessibility. And like always, you'll figure it out.
